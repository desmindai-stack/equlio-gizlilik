Equlio - Account and Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Data controller: Equlio (company incorporation in progress; controller details will be updated upon incorporation) · Data protection requests: kvkk@equlio.app

This page explains how users of the Equlio app can delete their account and the data associated with it.

From Within the App

You can delete your account yourself in the app via My profile → Delete my account permanently. For club owner accounts, because of the club's records, the deletion request is forwarded to the support team and completed within 30 days at the latest.

Request by Email

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to the address below saying that you want your account deleted.
  2. In your email, state the email address you are registered with in the app (for identity verification).
  3. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days at the latest after your request is received.
Email for account deletion requests:
destek@equlio.app
Subject: Account Deletion Request

What Data Is Deleted?

When your deletion request is approved, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your account information (first name, last name, email, phone)
  • Your membership and role information
  • Your rider and horse records
  • Your lesson and package history
  • Your personal notifications

What Data May Be Kept?

Because of legal obligations, completed payment / invoice records may be kept for the period required by the applicable financial legislation (usually 10 years). These records are kept only to meet legal requirements and are not used for any other purpose.
