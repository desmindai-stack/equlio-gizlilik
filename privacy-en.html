Equlio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Data controller: Equlio (company incorporation in progress; controller details will be updated upon incorporation) · Data protection requests: kvkk@equlio.app

At Equlio ("the app", "we") we care about your privacy. This privacy policy explains what information we collect when you use the Equlio app, how we use it and how we protect it. It has been prepared under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The Turkish version is the original text; this English version is a translation.

1. Information We Collect

To provide its riding-club management service, Equlio collects the following information:

  • Account information: First name, last name, email address, phone number.
  • Club and membership information: Role (club owner, trainer, groom, member), club membership status, riders and horse information.
  • Lesson and package information: Lesson schedules, package requests, attendance records.
  • Payment information: Payment amounts and status. Your card details are not stored by us; payments are processed through a secure payment provider (iyzico).
  • Health and care records: Health and care information about horses (entered only by the relevant authorised roles).

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used only for the following purposes:

  • Providing the club management service (members, lessons, payments, horse records)
  • Informing users about package approvals, payments, lesson changes and similar matters
  • Improving the service and keeping it secure

Your information is not used for advertising and is not sold to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Storage and Security

Your data is stored with our infrastructure provider Supabase, on servers in Germany (Frankfurt, AWS eu-central-1). Access is limited by role-based authorisation; each user can access only the information they are authorised to see. Your data is protected by appropriate technical and organisational measures.

5. Your Rights (KVKK Article 11)

Under KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information if it is, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, send your request to kvkk@equlio.app.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account from within the app (My profile → Delete my account permanently; for club owner accounts the deletion request is forwarded to the support team). Details: Account and data deletion. You can send KVKK requests about deleting your data to kvkk@equlio.app; your data is deleted within a reasonable time after your request.

7. Child Riders

Riders under 18 cannot open an Equlio account directly in their own name. A child rider is registered by their parent or legal guardian, and the record is managed from the parent's account.

  • Data processed: The child's first and last name and date of birth; riding level and (if the parent adds one) photo; lesson, horse, health and attendance records (e.g. lesson schedule, horse ridden, lesson and competition attendance).
  • Purpose: Only to provide the lesson and club service (lesson planning, horse assignment, attendance tracking and keeping the parent informed about these).
  • Who can see it: The parent; the authorised staff of the club where the child is registered, and the trainer giving the lessons.
  • Parent's rights: The parent can correct the child's information in the app (My profile → My riders). Requests to delete or correct it can be sent to kvkk@equlio.app.
